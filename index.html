The story

한공간 means one space. One room, one chef, one kitchen that behaves more like a laboratory — where tofu meets yuzu, lamb becomes a crisp pancake, and sea urchin turns up in a rosé pasta. Step off the crowded main street and come in.

Guests tend to find it by accident and come back on purpose. It's small, it's warm, the music is low, and dinner arrives a plate at a time to share.

Vegetarian and vegan guests are looked after — just say so when you book.
